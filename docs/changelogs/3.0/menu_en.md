**League, Expeditions & Rival**

## New

- **Windhaven**: new default starting island, a floating village. Sakura is still available.
- **9 trainer origins** (Ranger, Researcher, Battler, Breeder, Medic, Angler, Botanist, Hiker, Scavenger), each with its own skill tree.
- **Pokémon League** chapter: 12 Champions, the final Rival Kyusama and the League Trophy.
- **Expeditions** and **Rival Kyusama**: two new mods with their own quest chapters.
- **Gate quests**: one per rank, from E to Demon.
- **Minions** can be crafted again, with all 19 minion tools.
- **Lucky Blocks**: 33 special skin palettes.
- **Refined Storage** and **Creative** chapters rewritten.
- **Shops rebuilt**: one shop per category.

## Balance

- Skill XP now comes from your **vanilla XP**.
- Quest money rewards are now **coins**.
- Trainer prize money **divided by 10**.
- Loot gives **Poké Ball lids**. Poké Ball recipes are back to Pixelmon's originals.
- ProjectE: **no more EMC generation**.
- Silver and Gold Bottle Caps can no longer be crafted.
- New Mystery Bag loot tables.

## Fixes

- **29 Dungeons Arise structures** had empty chests: fixed.
- **Controls cleaned up**: PixelGO menus and origin skills moved to **Alt**, **P** opens the Pokénav. *Updating an old install? Reset your controls.*
- PixelGO window title and icon.
- Leftover example files removed.

## Mods added

- **NeoOrigins** + **NeoOrigins x Pufferfish's Skills Compat**: trainer origins and their skill trees.
- **PixelGO - Expedition** and **PixelGO - NPC** (Rival Kyusama).
- **More Common Gyms**: gyms spawn more often.
- **ProjectEMon** and **EMC Bridge**: EMC values for Pixelmon items.
- **Botany Pots - Mystical Agriculture Compat**: grow Mystical crops in Botany Pots.
- **Jade Addons**, **Legendary Tooltips**, **Prism**: better tooltips and info.
- **Drippy Loading Screen**: custom loading screen.
- **Better Compatibility Checker**: shows the pack version and warns if client and server differ.
- **Crash Assistant**: helps when the game crashes.
- Performance: **ModernFix**, **AllTheLeaks**, **Clumps**, **Packet Fixer**, **spark**.

## Mods removed

- **Integrated Dynamics**, **Integrated Terminals**, **Integrated Tunnels**.
- **Inventory Pets**.
- **ProjectExpansion** (EMC generation).
- **RFTools Storage**.
