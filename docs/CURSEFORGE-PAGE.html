🔥🚀 Soar to new heights with PixelGO - To the Sky 2! A Pixelmon skyblock RPG where you start on a floating island with almost nothing, and build your way up to the Pokémon League. 🔥🚀

 

 

🎮 Minecraft1.21.1 — NeoForge
💾 RAM8 GB recommended (6 GB minimum)
🌐 MultiplayerServer friendly — the Server Pack is in the Files tab
⏳ First launch2 to 3 minutes of loading is normal

 

 

🏝️ Start in the Sky 🏝️

Your adventure begins on a floating island! ☁️
Pick Windhaven, a floating village, or Sakura, a Japanese garden. Grow your first crops, unlock the cobblestone generator and expand from there. Every step has a quest, and every quest pays out!

 

⚙️ Minions ⚙️

Let your minions do the work! 🤖
Place a minion and it farms for you. Craft the tier I at a workbench, then upgrade it from inside the minion, tier after tier. Give it a tool, feed it Power Pots, and watch your production explode!

 

🚪 Gates 🚪

Only the strongest trainers make it out! ⚔️
Solo-Leveling style dungeon Gates, from rank E to S, SS, SSS and Demon. Each one ends with a boss, and clearing them is the only way to earn Aura Essence, the currency of endgame progression.

 

🏆 Battle Tower & Pokémon League 🏆

Prove you are the very best! 🥇
3 Battle Towers, 84 trainers and 84 badges to collect.
The Pokémon League: 12 Champions hidden on mountain peaks, one for each region. Beat them all, then face the final Rival — Kyusama, the creator of the pack!

 

🧭 Expeditions & the Rival 🧭

Your team never stops working! 🗺️
Send your Pokémon on expeditions to bring back resources while you play. Summon the Rival with a beacon and climb his tiers all the way up to Omega.

 

🎓 9 Trainer Origins 🎓

Choose your destiny! ⚡️🔥💧
Ranger, Researcher, Battler, Breeder, Medic, Angler, Botanist, Hiker or Scavenger: your origin unlocks its own skill tree. Your vanilla XP becomes skill XP, so everything you do makes you stronger.

 

🥚 Random Egg 🥚

Unleash the power of surprise! 🎁
Crack open a Random Egg and a random Pokémon egg joins your party. Will it hatch into a rare gem?

 

🎫 SSR Tickets 🎫

Catch a legend! 🎟️
Use an SSR Ticket to summon one of 71 legendary Pokémon — with a 4% chance of it being shiny ✨

 

🔑 Mega Key 🗝️

Unlock unparalleled strength! 💥
Summon a colossal Mega Pokémon boss among 59 Mega forms. Defeat it and its Mega Stone is yours!

 

💰 A Real Economy 💰

Farm, sell, get rich! 💸
Sell what you farm, buy from one shop per category, open Mystery Bags and break Pixelmon Lucky Blocks with 33 special-skin palettes. Quests pay in coins, and prices are balanced around your farm.

 

🏰 Structures & Raid Dens 🏰

There is a whole world beyond your island! 🗺️
Explore Dungeons Arise castles and towers, and find Raid Dens hiding powerful Pokémon. Their chests are filled with Pixelmon loot.

 

📜 700+ Quests 📜

A quest-driven adventure! 🌟🔍
Over 700 quests in 35 chapters guide you from your first log to the League. Recipes are in JEI, quests tell you what to do next.

 

🔧 Tech Mods 🔧

Harness the power of technology! ⚙️💡
Ex Deorum, Mekanism, Ender IO, Refined Storage, Mystical Agriculture, Extended Crafting, ProjectE (no EMC generation — you earn your power) and many more, all tied into the quest line.

 

 

📈 Progression 📈

Island → Farm → Cobblestone generator → Minions → Automation
→ Gates E to S → Battle Tower → Pokémon League → Singularity

❓ FAQ ❓

The game crashes while loading.
Give Minecraft at least 6 GB of RAM (8 GB recommended) in the CurseForge profile settings.

The loading screen is stuck.
The first launch takes 2 to 3 minutes. If it never ends, reinstall the pack from CurseForge and check that your antivirus did not block files in the instance folder.

Can I play solo?
Yes! Everything is designed for solo play, and works the same on a server.

Where are the recipes?
In JEI: press R on an item for its recipe, U for its uses.
